Our Story

Born from a love of baking & a jar of chocolate

CraveJar started in a home kitchen in Hyderabad, born from one simple idea — cookies are better when you dip them in chocolate. Not store-bought, not commercial. Real, warm, handcrafted cookies made with premium ingredients, packed with love and delivered fresh.

Every box is baked on the morning of your delivery. We never pre-bake. We never freeze. That's the CraveJar promise — if it's not fresh, it doesn't go out.

We're FSSAI certified, proudly based in Osman Nagar, Tellapur, Hyderabad. We're growing slowly, deliberately, and deliciously.
